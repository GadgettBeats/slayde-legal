Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This page explains how to have data held by Slayde deleted. Slayde is operated by Gadgett Beats Jan Rediger, a sole proprietorship registered in Poland.

There are three routes. Find the one that describes you and follow the steps.

  • Route 1, you have a Slayde account and want the whole account and its data deleted.
  • Route 2, you have a Slayde account and want one conversation deleted.
  • Route 3, you sent a direct message to a producer who uses Slayde, and you want your data deleted.

At the end of the page, section 4 explains what happens when you remove Slayde from inside Instagram, and section 5 is a summary table of what each route removes.

Contact address for every request on this page: gadgetbeats99@gmail.com

Route 1. Delete your Slayde account and all of its data

Use this if you have a Slayde account and want it gone.

Steps

  1. Sign in to Slayde.
  2. Select Account Settings in the left hand navigation.
  3. Go to the Danger Zone card at the bottom of the page.
  4. Select Delete account. A confirmation dialog opens.
  5. Type your account email address exactly as it appears on your account. The confirm button stays disabled until it matches.
  6. Confirm.

Deletion happens immediately. It cannot be undone, and your session ends.

What this removes

Your account record and everything attached to it, in a single operation:

  • your identity record, meaning your email address, your name, your internal account identifier and the date your account was created,
  • your sign-in identity at our authentication provider,
  • your stored Instagram connection: the encrypted access token, both Instagram account identifiers and the stored username,
  • every conversation, every message in it, whether received or sent, and the records of those sends,
  • the assessment fields and status labels stored per conversation, and the log of manual status changes,
  • your assistant configuration: persona, prompt text in draft and live form, and the full prompt version history,
  • your settings, including quiet hours, time zone, reply delays, follower threshold and your blocked accounts list,
  • your research tool data: playlist lookups, stored artist rows, table preferences and the cost records of your collections,
  • your lesson completion records,
  • your billing identifiers held in our database.

What this does not remove, stated plainly

  • One audit record survives, containing the email address of the deleted account, the IP address the deletion request came from and the browser user agent string. We keep it so we can demonstrate the deletion took place. If you want it removed as well, email us and we will remove it.
  • The shared copy of public artist information. The research tool keeps one shared copy of public artist information that is not linked to any account (privacy policy, section 7). Deleting your account does not remove it. An artist can ask us to remove their entry, see section 6.
  • Records held by Stripe. Deleting your account cancels your subscription, but the payment and invoice records Stripe holds stay with Stripe, which keeps them for its own accounting and legal obligations. To ask about those, contact Stripe.
  • Records held by Meta. Messages that travelled over Instagram exist in Instagram. Deleting your Slayde account does not delete anything inside Instagram. Use Instagram's own tools for that.
  • Records held by Google. Deleting your Slayde account removes your identity from our systems. Anything Google holds about your Google account is governed by Google's own policies, and you can review it in your Google account settings.

One honest caveat. If cancelling your subscription at Stripe fails at the moment of deletion, your account is still deleted. In that rare case the subscription could remain active at Stripe. If your account is gone and you are still being charged, email us and we will resolve it.

Route 2. Delete a single conversation

Use this if you want to remove one conversation rather than your whole account.

Steps

  1. Sign in to Slayde.
  2. Select Conversations in the left hand navigation.
  3. Open the conversation you want to remove.
  4. Select the bin icon in the conversation header, labelled Delete conversation, and confirm in the dialog.

What this removes

  • the contact record for that person, including their Instagram account identifier and any stored profile name,
  • every message in that conversation, both received and sent,
  • the delivery records for replies sent in that conversation,
  • the assessment fields and the status label stored for that conversation,
  • the status change log for that conversation.

All of it is removed together, immediately, and cannot be undone.

What this does not remove. The conversation as it exists inside Instagram. Deleting it in Slayde removes our copy only.

Route 3. You messaged a producer and want your data deleted

Use this if you sent a direct message to a music producer who uses Slayde and you want the data held about you removed.

Who decides. The producer you messaged controls that conversation. We store it for them. So there are two things you can do, and you can do both.

Step one, ask the producer. Contact the producer whose Instagram account you messaged and ask them to delete the conversation. They can do this themselves in a few seconds using Route 2. This is the fastest route.

Step two, or instead, write to us. Email gadgetbeats99@gmail.com and we will act on your request and notify the producer concerned.

Please include in your email:

  • the subject line Data deletion request,
  • your Instagram username, the one you messaged from,
  • the Instagram username of the producer or business you messaged,
  • the approximate date you sent the message.

We need these details to identify the right record. Conversations are stored against the account identifier Instagram gives us rather than against your username, so the more of the above you can give us, the faster we can locate your data. If we cannot identify it from what you send, we will write back and ask.

Our commitment. We respond within thirty days of receiving your request. We will confirm in writing when the deletion is done.

What we delete. Everything listed under "What this removes" in Route 2, for the conversation between you and that producer: your Instagram account identifier and any stored profile name, all message content in both directions, the delivery records, and the assessment fields.

If you left a comment rather than a message. Instagram may notify us about comments on a producer's posts, but we do not store anything from those notifications, so there is nothing on our side to delete. A comment itself lives in Instagram, and you can remove it there.

What we cannot do. We cannot delete the conversation from inside Instagram, and we cannot delete anything from the producer's own devices or their own records outside Slayde. For the copy inside Instagram, use Instagram's own tools.

4. If you removed Slayde from inside Instagram

You can revoke Slayde's access from within Instagram, under the settings for apps and websites connected to your account. You may also have used Instagram's data deletion request feature.

What happens automatically when you do this. We clear the stored connection to your Instagram account: the encrypted access token, both Instagram account identifiers and the stored username. After that, Slayde can no longer read or send messages for that account.

What this does not do, and this matters. Revoking access does not by itself delete conversations already stored in the panel. Messages received before the connection was revoked stay in the producer's inbox until the producer deletes them or deletes their account.

So if your intention was to delete the data, not only the connection, do one of these as well:

  • if the account is yours, follow Route 1 to delete your Slayde account and its conversations,
  • if you were the person messaging a producer, follow Route 3 and email us.

We would rather tell you this clearly than let you assume the data is gone when it is not.

6. Artists listed in the research tool

Slayde includes a research and discovery tool that lets a producer review information about artists associated with a Spotify playlist link. This tool does not send messages. It exists so a producer can look at artist information in a table.

The fields stored are: artist name, Spotify profile link, top track names, monthly listener counts and Instagram handle. No biography text, genre labels or email addresses are held. Part of this information is kept in one shared copy that is not linked to any producer account (privacy policy, section 7).

If you are an artist and want your entry removed, email gadgetbeats99@gmail.com with the subject line Data deletion request, your artist name and your Spotify profile link. We remove the entry from the shared copy and from every producer's table in Slayde. We respond within thirty days.
