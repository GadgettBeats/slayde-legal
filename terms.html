2. What the service is

Slayde is a web panel for music producers who receive direct messages on Instagram. When you connect your own Instagram professional account, Slayde can reply on your behalf to people who have sent you a message.

Replies are generated by an AI assistant that you configure. You write the persona and the instructions the assistant follows. You can read every conversation in the panel, switch the assistant off for any single conversation or for your whole account, and write replies yourself at any time.

Slayde replies inside conversations that another person has started. Every message it sends is a reply in such a conversation.

The panel also includes a research tool for reviewing artist information associated with a Spotify playlist link you submit, and learning material. The research tool does not send messages.
